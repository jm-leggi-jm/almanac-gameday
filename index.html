Almanac Game Day

The weather at every kickoff.

NFL games

Forecasts cover each game's window: kickoff through about three hours later, at the stadium. Dome games are listed last: Dome and Covered stadiums keep weather off the field (the outside forecast is shown for fans), and Retractable roofs are usually decided on game day. Each radar shows current conditions around that stadium. Times are in your computer's time zone.
