Parlays

Player-prop parlays

3, 4, 5 and 6 legs, player props only

How to read a card: Chance it hits comes from each player's past games; Break-even is how often the parlay must hit for the payout to be worth it, at the assumed −110 per leg. Past games usually make these look better than they are (⚠).

Built from DraftKings' player over/unders via ESPN (passing, rushing and receiving yards, and receptions) for this week's upcoming games, in all stadiums. Each prop takes the side the player's history favors: how often he cleared that line in his last 16 games (this season and last), pulled toward 50% so a small sample can't look like a sure thing. Players need at least 6 games. Only props whose line sits inside the player's normal range count (he cleared it in 20–80% of those games): lines far outside it are alternate lines, or signs the sportsbook knows about a new role or an injury. Tiny lines (under 1.5 receptions, 14.5 rushing or receiving yards, 149.5 passing yards) are skipped too. Legs are the likeliest first, with one per player and at most 2 per game.

* ESPN doesn't publish prop prices, so each leg is assumed to be −110 (the usual price for these lines). The real payout will differ, so check the sportsbook.

Checks each player's recent games (a few hundred lookups), then reuses the results for 6 hours.

Game parlays

4, 6, 8 and 10 legs, one moneyline or over/under per game

How these are built. This week's games that haven't started, in two groups: open-air stadiums only, and all stadiums (domes, covered and retractable roofs too). Each game adds one leg, a moneyline or an over/under, whichever has the higher estimated chance:

  • Moneyline: the average of ESPN's projection, Polymarket (when it's actually traded) and DraftKings' moneyline with the bookmaker's margin removed.
  • Over/under: DraftKings' total with the margin removed, averaged with Polymarket's over/under at the same line. For open-air games only, stadium weather adds a few points to the Under: strong wind +6, breezy +3, heavy rain or snow +5, rain likely +2 (capped at +10). That's a rule of thumb, and legs marked weather were affected. Indoor games get no weather adjustment: a fixed roof keeps it off the field, and retractable roofs are usually closed when it's bad.
  • Legs are ranked by estimated chance, and each parlay takes the top 4, 6, 8 or 10. Odds are DraftKings' via ESPN.
