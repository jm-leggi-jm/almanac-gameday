    Game day weather NFL

    Forecasts cover each game's window: kickoff through about three hours later, at the stadium. Roof types: Dome and Covered stadiums keep weather off the field (the outside forecast is shown for fans); Retractable roofs are usually decided on game day. Radar shows current conditions around each stadium. Schedules from ESPN; times in your computer's time zone.

    Observed and forecast data: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Almanac calls are entered by you; this app isn't affiliated with the Farmers' Almanac.
