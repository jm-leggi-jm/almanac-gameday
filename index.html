Almanac Game Day

The weather at every kickoff.

NFL games

Forecasts cover each game's window: kickoff through about three hours later, at the stadium. Live games come first; within live and upcoming, dome games are listed last: Dome and Covered stadiums keep weather off the field (the outside forecast is shown for fans). A retractable roof shows the note “retractable roof, usually closed in bad weather” and gets no weather adjustment. Open-air and retractable-roof games show a radar of current conditions around the stadium (folded under Show radar when the weather risk is low); dome games don’t. Times are in your computer's time zone.

Odds & projections: sportsbook lines via ESPN, ESPN's Matchup Predictor, and Polymarket prices, shown for information only. Prices and lines change constantly. 21+. Gambling problem? Call 1-800-GAMBLER.

Past games

Every game played this season from Week 1, newest week first, and within a week open-air games first. Weather is what the models recorded at the stadium during each game's window (kickoff through about three hours later). Odds are the last ones before kickoff, and Result vs. the line shows who covered the spread and whether the total went over. Details load as you scroll.

Parlays

Player-prop parlays

3, 4, 5 and 6 legs, player props only

How to read a card: Chance it hits is a market estimate, no edge implied. A leg with both sides priced uses that price with the vig removed. A leg without a price prints assumed −110 and counts as 50%. Break-even uses the posted or assumed price, vig included.

Built from DraftKings' player over/unders via ESPN (passing, rushing and receiving yards, and receptions) for the upcoming games you pick this week (all by default), in any stadium. When both sides have an American price (any field whose name looks like a price, and only a number ≤ −100 or ≥ +100), the card takes the favored side of that de-vigged price. Otherwise the side comes from how often he cleared that line in his last 16 games (this season and last), pulled toward 50% so a small sample can't look like a sure thing. That history is shown on the leg and used to order the card. It is not the hit chance when the price is assumed. Players need at least 6 games. Only props whose line sits inside the player's normal range count (he cleared it in 20–80% of those games): lines far outside it are alternate lines, or signs the sportsbook knows about a new role or an injury. Tiny lines (under 1.5 receptions, 14.5 rushing or receiving yards, 149.5 passing yards) are skipped too. Legs are ordered likeliest first, with one per player and at most 2 per game (more when you pick only a few games, so the big parlays can fill). Two legs from the same game are flagged; the hit chance still multiplies them.

assumed −110 is printed on every leg whose feed has no usable American odds. Those legs are 50% in the hit chance (what −110 on both sides is after the vig comes out) and are not a real estimate. The payout still uses −110, vig included. Check the sportsbook.

Checks each player's recent games (a few hundred lookups), then reuses the results for 6 hours.

Game parlays

4, 6, 8 and 10 legs, one moneyline or over/under per game

How these are built. This week's games that haven't started, in two groups: open-air stadiums only, and all stadiums (domes, covered and retractable roofs too). Each game adds one leg, a moneyline or an over/under, whichever favorite has the higher fair chance. The card starts on favored sides. Other side switches that leg to the underdog.

  • Books: ESPN's posted odds are DraftKings, the same book as the book feed, so that book is counted once. The Matchup Predictor stays on the game card and is not blended in. If more books are posted, their de-vigged prices are averaged, or the median when there are three or more.
  • Polymarket is divided so the two sides add up (home and away, or over and under). That is not sportsbook vig math. The moneyline is blended 70% books / 30% Polymarket only when it has traded at least $10,000 and, when a liquidity figure is posted, has at least $10,000 available. A total is used only at the book's number, and only when that line has traded at least $2,500 (and has at least $5,000 available when liquidity is posted). Otherwise the leg uses the books only. The blend is a weighted average.
  • Weather is a flag only. It does not change the chance. Domes, covered stadiums, and retractable roofs get no numeric adjustment: ATL, DAL, HOU, IND, ARI, DET, NO, MIN, LV, and SoFi (LAR/LAC). A retractable roof is noted “retractable roof, usually closed in bad weather”.
  • Payout uses the posted American odds, vig included. Chance it hits multiplies the de-vigged leg prices. That is a market estimate, no edge implied. A tie usually pushes a moneyline out of the parlay; the math ignores that.

Sunday afternoon window (4:05 & 4:25 PM ET), all stadiums

The late-afternoon slate only: games kicking off 4:00–4:59 PM Eastern that haven't started, in any stadium. It's usually 3–5 games, so these are 2-, 3- and 4-leg parlays.

Open-air stadiums only

How to read a card: Chance it hits is a market estimate, no edge implied. Break-even is how often the posted payout, vig included, must hit to get the stake back. Favored legs are labeled. Other side is a pick you made.

All stadiums, including domes and retractable roofs

For information only, not betting advice. Chance it hits is a market estimate, no edge implied. It multiplies each leg's de-vigged price, as if the legs were independent. Break-even is the chance the combined payout implies, which includes the sportsbook's margin on every leg. A tie usually pushes that moneyline out of the parlay; ties are left out of the math. Parlays rarely hit, and a longer parlay is far less likely to. Lines move constantly. Check the sportsbook before betting. 21+. Gambling problem? Call 1-800-GAMBLER.

Change log

What's changed in Almanac Game Day, newest first. Times are Central.

October 7, 2026

12:20 PM CT

  • Prop prices: an over and an under written as plain numbers inside an odds object are both kept, with their sides. A repeated copy of the same price is still read once.

12:17 PM CT

  • Review round 5: over and under prices inside an odds object are both kept, with their sides. A second, different price is kept too. Only a repeated copy of the same price is skipped.

12:13 PM CT

  • Review round 4: when one odds object repeats the same price under more than one name, such as value and american, that price is read once.

12:10 PM CT

  • Prop prices: a nested price such as odds.american is read once. One real price no longer looks like two and falls back to assumed −110.

12:03 PM CT

  • Polymarket totals: every total line that has enough trading is kept, and the parlay uses the one at the sportsbook’s number. A busier line at a different total no longer crowds the matching one out.
  • When a price counts: an id such as oddsId is not read as American odds, a price nested under a name like american.value is, and a number past 10,000 is ignored. A blank volume is not treated as zero. Books are matched by the sportsbook’s id.
  • Floors: a Polymarket moneyline needs at least $10,000 traded and $10,000 available. Each total line needs at least $2,500 traded and, when liquidity is posted, $5,000 available.
  • Tracker and weather: when the tie is under 1%, the away share is 100 minus the home share. Below 20°F uses the air temperature.

11:50 AM CT

  • Parlay chances: book odds have the vig removed and the legs are multiplied. ESPN’s posted line is DraftKings, so that book is counted once, then blended 70/30 with Polymarket only when the market has real volume and liquidity. The auto card is favorites. The payout uses the posted odds, vig included, and the card says the hit chance is a market estimate, no edge implied. Weather no longer changes the chance.
  • Prop prices: a real American price is used when both sides have one. Otherwise the leg prints “assumed −110” and counts as 50%. Two props from the same game are flagged.
  • Weather: domes, covered stadiums, and retractable roofs get no numeric adjustment. Retractable roofs say “retractable roof, usually closed in bad weather.” Rain uses Open-Meteo’s rain and showers, not total precipitation. Wind flags start at 15 mph or a 25 mph gust. Snow is a flag only. Below 20°F is its own cold flag.
  • Tracker: the away team’s win share is what’s left after the home team and a tie. The tie is shown when it is at least 1%.

11:24 AM CT

  • Weather requests: stadium forecasts are still one download per venue, reused for 30 minutes. Starts are spaced out with a short random pause, and a “too many requests” reply now waits (a little jitter, and at least as long as the server asks) before trying again. The offline cache no longer hides that reply, so the wait actually happens. A damaged saved forecast is fetched again instead of breaking the card.
  • Phones: the tracker sits above the games instead of squeezing them into a sliver. The team picker is one column, parlay legs wrap so the pick isn’t cut off, and the search box uses a larger type size so phones don’t zoom in on it.
  • When something fails: the tracker says “Update failed, retrying…” instead of leaving the old time up. Prop parlays say how many lookups failed. A Polymarket link keeps the address the site meant even if the slug contains odd characters. A missing card section no longer stops the rest of the card from filling in.
