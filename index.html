NFL games

Forecasts cover each game's window: kickoff through about three hours later, at the stadium. Dome games are listed last: Dome and Covered stadiums keep weather off the field (the outside forecast is shown for fans), and Retractable roofs are usually decided on game day. Open-air and retractable-roof games show a radar of current conditions around the stadium; dome games don't. Times are in your computer's time zone.

Odds & projections: sportsbook lines via ESPN, ESPN's Matchup Predictor, and Polymarket prices, shown for information only. Prices and lines change constantly. 21+. Gambling problem? Call 1-800-GAMBLER.

Open-air parlays

How these are built. Only this week's games that haven't started, in open-air stadiums (no domes, covered or retractable roofs). Each game adds one leg, a moneyline or an over/under, whichever has the higher estimated chance:

  • Moneyline: the average of ESPN's projection, Polymarket (when it's actually traded) and DraftKings' moneyline with the bookmaker's margin removed.
  • Over/under: DraftKings' total with the margin removed, averaged with Polymarket's over/under at the same line. Stadium weather adds a few points to the Under: strong wind +6, breezy +3, heavy rain or snow +5, rain likely +2 (capped at +10). That's a rule of thumb, and legs marked weather were affected.
  • Legs are ranked by estimated chance, and each parlay takes the top 4, 6, 8 or 10. Odds are DraftKings' via ESPN.

For information only, not betting advice. "Our estimate" multiplies each leg's chance, as if games were independent; "Priced at" is the chance the combined odds imply, including the sportsbook's margin. Parlays rarely hit, and a longer parlay is far less likely to. Lines move constantly. Check the sportsbook before betting. 21+. Gambling problem? Call 1-800-GAMBLER.
